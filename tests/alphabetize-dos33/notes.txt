ALPHABETIZE CATALOG (DOS 3.3)

Notes:

ps-graphics-DOS33.do is a DOS 3.3 disk with a large number of small files.

Requirements:

- two disk drives in slot 6

Steps:

- Duplicate ps-graphics-DOS33.do to sorted-v84.do
- Launch Copy ][ Plus v8.4
- Mount sorted-v84.do in Slot 6, Drive 2
- Alphabetize Catalog > select Slot 6, Drive 2 > [G]o
- Quit Copy ][ Plus
- Duplicate ps-graphics-DOS33.do to sorted-reboot.do
- Launch Copy ][ Reboot
- Mount sorted-reboot.do in Slot 6, Drive 2
- Alphabetize Catalog > select Slot 6, Drive 2 > [G]o
- Quit Copy ][ Reboot

Expectations:

- Once each alphabetize operation starts, there are no further prompts before main menu appears
